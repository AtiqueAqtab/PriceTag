# Getting started

Build and run PriceTag, then make your first page.

## Requirements

- Xcode 15 or later
- iOS 16 or later

## Build

1. Open `PriceTag.xcodeproj`.
2. Choose your team under Signing & Capabilities.
3. Press ⌘R.

## Make a page

1. On the **Pages** tab, tap **+** to create a page.
2. Tap an empty slot and choose a sign type.
3. Fill in the fields. The preview at the top updates as you type.
4. Tap **Export** to preview the sheet, then share it to Print, Files or AirDrop.

Pages with fewer than four signs repeat what they have to fill the sheet.
