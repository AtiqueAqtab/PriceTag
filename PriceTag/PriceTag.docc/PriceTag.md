# ``PriceTag``

Make printable price signs, four to a US Letter page.

## Overview

PriceTag replaces a Word-based sign workflow at a motorcycle dealership. Staff pick a sign type, fill in a few fields, and export a vector PDF ready to print, laminate and cut.

The same view draws the sign on screen and in the PDF, so the preview always matches the printout.
